Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2026

Welcome to CreatorKit. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

CreatorKit currently provides browser-based creator tools. Text entered into our generators and images processed by our image tools are handled in your browser.

We do not currently require users to create accounts or provide personal information to use our tools.

2. How We Use Information

We may use basic website usage information to understand how visitors interact with CreatorKit and improve the website.

3. Cookies

CreatorKit itself does not currently require cookies for its basic tools. Third-party services, if added in the future, may use cookies or similar technologies.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services in the future, including analytics and advertising providers. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of our website. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

CreatorKit is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CreatorKit develops. Changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the CreatorKit website owner.
